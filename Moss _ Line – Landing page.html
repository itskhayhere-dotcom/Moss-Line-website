Questions, answered

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app and start planning right away.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser on your own device. Nothing needs to be sent to a server.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Each main screen has an Export to PDF button.

How do I install it on Android?

Download the APK, open it, and allow installs from your browser if Android asks. Then tap Install.

Where can I use Moss / Line?

Download it for iPhone and iPad or for Android, or open it on the web in any browser.
